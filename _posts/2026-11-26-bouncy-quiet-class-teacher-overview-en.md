When a classroom gets loud, constant reminders wear down both the teacher and the students. [Quiet Class (Bouncy)](https://sivochka.com/bouncy/) is a free browser noise meter built for classroom TVs, projectors and smart boards. It requires no sign-up or installation, and after the first visit it keeps working offline.

## One-minute setup before the bell

You only need a computer with a browser and any connected microphone.

1. Open [sivochka.com/bouncy/](https://sivochka.com/bouncy/) on the computer connected to your board, projector or TV.
2. Click **Start Lesson** and allow microphone access in the browser bar.
3. Pick your class in the class list window or add a new one.
4. Ask the class to stay quiet for three seconds and press the target button ◎ next to the traffic light to calibrate the room's baseline noise.
5. Press **F** for fullscreen.

If sunlight hits the board or your projector is dim, turn on Light room in settings. You can also install the app to your desktop so it opens in its own window without typing the address each time.

## How Quiet Class works across three modes and background tabs

You can switch how strict the meter is with one click at the top or keys 1, 2 and 3.

| Mode | Best for | Sound reaction and stars |
|---|---|---|
| Quiet Work (1) | Independent work, tests, reading | Strictest: reacts even to loud whispering, earns stars |
| Group Work (2) | Pair and group tasks | Allows low talking, alerts only on loud chatter, earns stars |
| Break / Classic (3) | Breaks, warm-ups, short fun pauses | Balls bounce with voice volume, no stars earned |

A practical detail for real lessons: switching to a presentation or gradebook tab does not pause the meter. In a background tab it keeps listening, awarding stars and running the task timer, while the browser tab title shows the current zone, remaining time and a colored status dot.

When the room gets too loud, a gentle alert sounds. There are 9 built-in soft sounds (shush, chime, music box, owl, water drop, breath, radar, bell, arcade) or No sound. The first alert plays quietly at 45% of your chosen volume and steps up only if the noise continues. The meter ignores its own speaker sounds so it does not trigger itself. You can also upload up to 12 custom MP3 or WAV clips (trimmed to 5 seconds) and assign a separate alert to each class.

## Star jar, class list and task timer

The reward loop lets students see progress right away without losing what they already earned. One star flies into the jar for every 30, 60 or 90 seconds of quiet (configured per class). The yellow zone pauses progress, and the red zone resets only the star currently being earned. **Earned stars are never taken away.** Two minutes of unbroken quiet trigger a Quiet streak.

The draggable class window holds multiple classes or clubs at once, each with its own jar, goal, custom reward (such as 5 minutes of a cartoon or a board game) and dated prize history. A leaderboard button sorts classes by all-time stars with medals for the top three. You can drag the window anywhere on the board with a mouse or finger.

For timed exercises, the **Timer** button offers presets from 1 to 20 minutes or any custom duration up to 90 minutes, with large digits visible from the back row. Finishing a timer adds one completed task, and finishing without hitting the red zone adds a bonus star.

## 13 character themes that read at a glance

Characters show the room state without words: they sleep when it is quiet, wake up and look suspicious on whispers, and bounce in panic when it gets loud. There are 13 themes in the ball settings: critters (default), pixel craft cubes, blocky heroes, arena brawlers, elemental creatures, capybaras, kawaii sweets, space planets, soap bubbles (which can be popped by touch on a smart board), emoji, glossy balls, numbers 1–10 and cursor-following eyes. You can also change ball count (10 to 150) and size (0.2× to 2.0×). Switching themes keeps all stars intact, so letting a class pick the week's theme makes an easy mini-reward.

## Offline use and moving classes between rooms

All classes, stars and settings stay locally in the browser. If you teach in several rooms or switch computers, open backups in settings and click **Save file** to export a `.json` backup onto a flash drive, then restore it on another computer in one click. The app also keeps the last 5 automatic snapshots after major actions (adding or deleting a class, hitting a goal or resetting a jar) so an accidental click will not wipe progress.
