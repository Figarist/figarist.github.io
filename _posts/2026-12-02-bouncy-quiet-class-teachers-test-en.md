I have already used [Quiet Class (Bouncy)](https://sivochka.com/bouncy/) in a noisy classroom along with its built-in reward system. It works, but my own room and hardware are only one setup. That is why I am opening an ongoing call for anyone who wants to test Quiet Class in real lessons and send honest feedback.

If you have not seen the app yet, I covered all modes, the timer, themes and the one-minute setup in the previous post on [what Quiet Class (Bouncy) is and how it helps in lessons](https://sivochka.com/blog/bouncy-quiet-class-teacher-overview/). This post is strictly about testing, known rough edges and how to reach me.

## Why I need real practice across different rooms

Microphones behave differently from one setup to another. A laptop microphone on the teacher's desk, a webcam mounted above a TV and a separate desk mic each pick up student voices, footsteps and room noise in their own way.

I need hands-on practice: teachers running the tool on different devices for at least a couple of lessons in different conditions. That is the only reliable way to see where sensitivity feels right and where it gets in the way.

## An honest problem I already know about

Right now the meter reacts very strongly to sounds coming from the computer itself. If the PC plays a notification sound or audio from a video on the speakers, the meter triggers hard right away. That is a real issue, and I do not want to hide it.

Until I improve that inside the app, there is one working habit: **before playing any video or audio on the computer, press Space (pause) or M (mute microphone)**. If anything else like this pops up in your classroom, those are exactly the details I want to hear about.

## How participation and privacy work

There is no seat limit and no deadline. You do not need to fill out a form or wait for approval.

| Detail | How it works |
|---|---|
| Who can join | Teachers, curators, club leaders and anyone interested |
| Spots and deadline | Unlimited spots, open indefinitely |
| What you do | Run it for at least a couple of lessons in different conditions and send short feedback |
| Cost and what you get | Completely free; your suggestions directly shape how I update the tool |
| Classroom privacy | Audio is analyzed locally in the browser only; nothing is recorded or sent to any server |

One privacy request for your message: **please do not send student names, photos or audio recordings**. I only need your observations and basic details about your device.

## How to test Quiet Class and where to write

Open [sivochka.com/bouncy/](https://sivochka.com/bouncy/), try it over a couple of lessons and message me directly wherever is easiest for you:

- Telegram: [t.me/Figarist](https://t.me/Figarist)
- Email: [igorsivoch@gmail.com](mailto:igorsivoch@gmail.com)

Write in plain language. Three short notes are especially helpful:

1. Which device and microphone you used (laptop, PC with webcam, smart board, etc.).
2. What genuinely helped during the lesson and what triggered too often or got in the way.
3. What was missing and what you would change first.

I am open to any requests and always glad to hear from teachers. Everyone who writes back after testing genuinely influences this tool, and I will keep adjusting it around real classroom needs.
